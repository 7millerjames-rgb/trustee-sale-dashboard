Privacy Policy (SMS Program)

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Cadence ("we," "us") operates an SMS alert service that notifies subscribers when relevant information becomes available. This policy describes how we handle information connected to that SMS program.

Information We Collect

When you opt in to receive alerts, we collect your mobile phone number and any additional information you provide at that time (such as your name, email, or alert preferences).

How We Use Your Information

We use your mobile number solely to deliver the SMS alerts you've requested and to manage your subscription (including processing opt-out and help requests). We do not use this information for unrelated marketing.

No Sharing of Mobile Information

We do not share, sell, rent, or otherwise disclose mobile phone numbers or SMS opt-in data to third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes. Your mobile number is used only to deliver the alerts you signed up for.

Message Frequency

Message frequency varies and is triggered by qualifying events rather than a fixed schedule. You may receive up to several messages per month depending on your alert settings.

Message and Data Rates

Message and data rates may apply.

Opt-Out and Help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us directly (below).

Data Retention & Security

We retain your mobile number only as long as needed to provide the alert service and take reasonable measures to protect it from unauthorized access.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected here with an updated effective date.
